How to read it

Crates depend downward. The hosts at the top link everything they need; oasis-types at the foundation links nothing; the backends sit below the trait boundary and implement its traits.

  • Hover / Tab a crate: its edges
  • Click / Enter: pin it here
  • ← →: next crate · Esc: clear

Generated from every Cargo.toml by scripts/gen-crate-map.py.
