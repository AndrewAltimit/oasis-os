Welcome to OASIS_OS!
====================

This is your home directory. Have a look around:

  /home/user/Documents/   Your text files and notes.
  /home/user/Music/       Sample tracks — try the Music Player.
  /home/user/Pictures/    Sample images — try the Photo Viewer.
  /home/user/scripts/     Shell scripts you can run in the Terminal.

Tips
----
- Open the Dashboard (Start menu) to browse installed apps.
- Highlight a file in File Manager and press Confirm to open it
  in the right viewer (Photo Viewer, Music Player, or Text Editor).
- Press Cancel to back out of any app.

Have fun!
