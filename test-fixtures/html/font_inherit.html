Font Shorthand

Full shorthand: italic, bold, 11px, 1.6 line-height.
font: 14px monospace
Size + family shorthand only.
font: italic 10px serif
Style + size + family shorthand.

Inheritance

Parent: "HeadingFont", Georgia, serif at 12px
Child inherits family, adds bold weight.
Child overrides to monospace at 9px.

Deep Nesting

Level 1: "HeadingFont", serif at 11px
Level 2: inherits family, bold weight
Level 3: inherits family+bold, adds italic
Level 3 override: monospace, no italic
